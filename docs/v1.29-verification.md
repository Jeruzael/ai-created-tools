## Self-Review Findings

The baseline findings and pre-edit plan are in
[the audit](v1.29-hardening-audit.md). Adversarial self-review additionally found:

- Overflowing JSON exponents bypassed the initial non-finite-number rejection.
- Missing scan roots could become the working directory after `abspath`.
- String/integer acknowledgements were incorrectly accepted as explicit consent.
- Malformed coverage ledgers could contradict sources, selection or scope and
  appear complete, or crash UI/history projection.
- Nested legacy record fields needed stronger validation before summary logic.
- Socket timeouts alone did not bound a slow-drip request's total admission time.
- HTTP shutdown needed to reject other in-flight handlers before committing exit.
- Thread-lock contention needed to share the allowlist transaction deadline.
- Empty test discovery could otherwise pass verification.

Resolution status: **fixed and retested**. The final 195-test suite and ten native
checks pass. Automatic verification also now leaves network/device and ambiguous
relative references explicitly unverified rather than probing them. These are
internal review findings, not an independent assessment.

## Remaining Boundaries

- Same-user local processes are not authenticated by the browser CSRF controls.
- Windows permissions, installed providers and source availability vary by host.
- Collection and cancellation are cooperative, not forced interruption of Windows calls.
- Native signature success is unavailable in this environment; see the module
  limitation above. No missing source is presented as complete inspection.
- Referenced-path guards do not eliminate local reparse/remapping races.
- Atomic replacement is not universal power-loss durability; forced termination
  can leave a non-authoritative private temporary file.
- Cooperating allowlist writers are serialized; independent dashboard processes
  do not share a job scheduler.
- Historical missing coverage remains not recorded, never retroactively complete.
- Parent-folder README/ignore-rule changes are local to a separate uncommitted checkout;
  deliverable docs/code/CI are committed in this application repository.
