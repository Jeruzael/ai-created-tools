Scan safety and scope

This tool reads files and writes its own local reports; it does not delete or modify scanned files. Start with C:\Users for a smaller review. Full-drive scans take longer and may skip protected folders.

  • Review large folders manually. Use official uninstallers or Windows cleanup tools where appropriate.
  • Never delete Windows, Program Files, ProgramData or AppData contents just because they are large.
  • Do not randomly delete Unity Assets, ProjectSettings or Packages folders.
  • Unknown processes are not automatically harmful.
  • Reports contain private paths and filenames. Keep them private.
  • Close heavy apps when practical. Elevated access can change visibility; Administrator behavior is not verified for this preview. Do not bypass permission or policy restrictions.
